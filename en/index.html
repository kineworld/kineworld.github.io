01 / The name and mark

Why KineWorld

The name speaks to surveying and understanding environments, spaces and boundaries.

We study how states change, what actions may cause and where possible futures may lead.

About KineWorld ↗

02 / What is a world model?

From seeing to understanding change.

Perception asks, “What is here now?” A world model asks, “What might happen if I take this action?”

Prediction is never certainty. Research must record assumptions, failures and boundaries.
