ABOUT

Why KineWorld

From the known toward wider possibilities.

The Chinese name 勘境 combines surveying and understanding with environment, space and boundaries. Our interest extends beyond a single image to how states change and what actions might cause.

THE MARK

A spatial mark

The mark evokes a higher-dimensional spatial structure: an entrance to deeper understanding. It is a brand metaphor, not a claim about a mathematically defined object.

THE PORTAL

A door to the unknown

From observation to understanding, from the current state to possible futures. The portal expresses curiosity and care in the face of uncertainty.
